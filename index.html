About Me

Mira is a freshman at Princeton University studying Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering with a minor in Creative Writing.

She graduated high school with a 3.96 GPA and a 1560 SAT score, and was admitted to multiple highly selective programs, including the prestigious Iowa Young Writers' Workshop and the Kenyon Young Writers' Studio.

In high school, Mira served as Upper School President, led multiple student organizations, and worked closely with nonprofits and national organizations. She has extensive experience mentoring others—having taught Python classes, tutored students in both math and English, and worked as a camp counselor for children.
